**Fellow Hero is ready for a first look: https://fellow2.dhapps.ai**

We pay a lot for Fellow every month, and most of us use one part of it: notes, 1:1 history, and action items. Fellow Hero rebuilds that part in house at zero per seat. I would like a handful of people to actually use it for a week and tell me where it falls short.

Sign in with your Delivery Hero account. Nothing to install.

**What works today**

- **Your calendar, synced.** Connect Google once in Settings and your meetings appear, refreshing on their own. Declined meetings show struck through, all day events and personal blocks included, the same as Google.
- **A note per meeting** with the three fixed blocks Fellow uses: Talking Points, Action Items, Notepad.
- **Per person 1:1 history.** Open anyone in People and see every 1:1 you have had with them, ranked by who you actually meet.
- **One unified action list** across every meeting, with filters, a back link to the source note, and My items versus Assigned to others.
- **Carry forward.** Unfinished items from your last meeting with the same people appear at the top of the next one.
- **Search everything** with Cmd+K: meetings, notes, action items, people.
- **Create meetings** from the calendar, including writing them to your real Google Calendar.
- **Desktop reminders** five minutes before a meeting, even with the browser closed. Opt in under Settings.
- **@ someone** in an action item to assign it. Drag rows to reorder.
- **A shared Notepad.** If we were both invited to a meeting, we get the same Notepad on it and can type in it at the same time. It syncs live and shows who else is looking. See the note below about what this does and does not cover.

**What is deliberately not there**

- **No AI, no recording, no transcription, no Ask Fellow.** This was cut on purpose. You write the notes.
- **Shared editing covers the Notepad only.** Talking Points and Action Items are still your own copy, so you cannot build an agenda together before a meeting the way Fellow lets you.
- **No rich text.** The notepad does bullets, indenting and nesting, but no bold or italic.
- **No templates, no favourites, no note tabs, no export, no comments.**
- **Guest search only finds people you have met.** Searching the full company directory needs a Google permission we have not requested yet.
- **Avatars are initials, not real photos.** Same reason.

**Most useful things to try**

1. **Use it for a real day.** Take notes in actual meetings rather than clicking around. That is the only way the gaps show up.
2. **Live in the Actions tab.** Does it genuinely replace how you track follow ups today, or is something missing?
3. **Open a recurring 1:1** and see whether carry forward puts the right things in front of you.
4. **Search for something you half remember.** That is where an in house tool usually disappoints.
5. **Turn on reminders** and tell me whether they arrive and whether five minutes is the right warning.
6. **Try the shared Notepad with someone.** Find a meeting you both attended, open it at the same time, and type. Tell me whether it feels live, whether you lose keystrokes, and whether seeing someone else in there is useful or unsettling.
7. **Compare it side by side with Fellow** on the same meeting and tell me what you miss. Screenshots are the most useful thing you can send.

**What is shared and what is not**

This is the one thing worth reading twice, because it is different from how the app behaved last week.

- **The Notepad is shared with everyone on the calendar invite** who also uses Fellow Hero. If six people were invited to a meeting, all six get the same Notepad on it. Membership comes from the invite itself, so it happens automatically and there is currently no way to remove someone from a note.
- **Talking Points and Action Items are not shared.** Those stay your own copy.
- **Private notes on a 1:1 are never shared.** That panel exists precisely for things the other person should not see, and it never leaves your account.

The practical version: treat the Notepad as if the other attendees can read it, because they can. Anything you would not say in the meeting belongs in Private notes.

**Three things to know before you click**

- **Creating an event really does create it.** It writes to your actual Google Calendar and sends real invitations to anyone you add, exactly as Fellow does. Deleting a meeting cancels it in Google too. Please do not experiment with this on colleagues who are not in this group.
- **This is a prototype.** Your notes are scoped to you and nobody else can read them, but it has not been through an InfoSec review. Use your judgement about anything genuinely sensitive, particularly in 1:1 notes.

Anything broken, confusing or missing, send it my way. Rough reactions are as useful as bug reports.
