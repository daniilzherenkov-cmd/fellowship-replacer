# Fellow 2 — quick start (test build)

Hi Milena! This is an early test build of **Fellow 2** — a native macOS replacement for
Fellow's notes / 1:1s / action-items, running entirely **on your Mac** (nothing is uploaded).

It takes ~3 minutes to set up. Thanks for trying it 🙏

---

## 1. Install (one time)

1. **Unzip** the file I sent (`Fellow2-….zip`) — double-click it.
2. Drag **Fellow 2** into your **Applications** folder.
3. Double-click **Fellow 2**. macOS will block it with a dialog (e.g. *"Fellow 2 not
   opened"* with **Done** / **Move to Trash**). This is expected — it only means the app
   isn't from the App Store. Click **Done** (do **not** choose *Move to Trash*).
4. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down to the **Security** section.
   You'll see *"Fellow 2 was blocked to protect your Mac"* → click **Open Anyway** →
   authenticate with Touch ID / password → if it asks once more, click **Open Anyway** again.
   *(Don't see that message? Double-click Fellow 2 once more, then re-check Settings. Still
   stuck? Ping Danya.)*
5. Fellow 2 opens. 🎉 (You only do steps 3–4 once.)

> Requires **macOS 14 (Sonoma) or newer**. Check: Apple menu  → About This Mac.

---

## 2. Connect your calendar (so your real meetings show up)

The Google sign-in button isn't wired up yet, so for now we read your calendar through
macOS — which works great and stays private:

1. **System Settings → Internet Accounts → Add Account → Google** → sign in with your
   **@deliveryhero.com** account. Make sure **Calendars** is turned on.
2. In **Fellow 2**, top-right **Sync ▾ → Sync macOS Calendar**.
3. Click **Allow** when macOS asks for Calendar access.

Your meetings (±3 months) will appear in the **Calendar** tab. Re-run **Sync** anytime to refresh.

---

## 3. What to try

- **Calendar** — your agenda; click the date to jump to any day, or switch to **Week**.
- Open a meeting → write **Talking Points**, tick **Action Items**, jot in the **Notepad**.
- Assign an action item to someone (the avatar on the right, or type **@**).
- **People** — click a person to see your shared 1:1 history + their open to-dos.
- **Actions** — every action item across all meetings in one list.
- **Meetings** — the full archive of all meetings by date.

---

## What's intentionally not here yet (v1)

- **No AI / transcription** — notes are written by hand (by design for now).
- **No Google sign-in button** — use *Sync macOS Calendar* above instead.
- **Local only** — your data lives on your Mac; there's no sharing between people yet.
